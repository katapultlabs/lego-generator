## Data

- Brick colors: RGB values and color codes from the [LDraw](https://www.ldraw.org) `LDConfig.ldr`
  (LDraw.org Parts Library). Exported `.ldr` files reference LDraw parts by number.
- LDraw → BrickLink color ids follow [Rebrickable](https://rebrickable.com/colors/)'s color table,
  checked against BrickLink's color guide.
